👏 Clap Finder

Privacy Policy

Effective: 27 October 2026

Clap Finder is designed to detect a clap on your device and play the alert you select. The app performs sound detection locally on your phone.

Information we collect

Clap Finder does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The current version does not include advertising or analytics services.

Microphone and audio

The microphone permission is used while clap detection is active. Audio is analysed in real time on your device to identify a clap. The app does not save recordings or send microphone audio to a server.

Other device permissions

  • Notifications: shows that detection is active and gives you controls for the running service.
  • Camera/flash: controls the device torch when the flash alert option is enabled. The app does not take photos or videos.
  • Vibration: vibrates the device when the selected alert uses vibration.

Settings stored on your device

Your selected sound, sensitivity, language, schedule, and related preferences are stored locally on your device. Removing the app normally removes this local app data.

Data security and retention

Because the current version does not transmit personal information or microphone recordings to us, we do not retain that data on our servers.

Children’s privacy

Clap Finder is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe information has been provided to us, contact the developer so the concern can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when that happens.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer contact details shown on Clap Finder’s Google Play listing.
